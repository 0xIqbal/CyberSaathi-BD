ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
High blood pressure, or hypertension, means the force of blood against the artery walls is persistently too high. Many people with hypertension have no symptoms, which is why regular blood-pressure checks are important.
In Bangladesh, IEDCR cites a national study estimating hypertension prevalence at 26.4 percent in 2011. Risk can be higher with overweight or obesity, diabetes, and other health conditions.

IMPORTANT WARNINGS:
Seek immediate care if blood pressure is very high—usually 180/120 or higher—and there is severe headache, chest pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty, nausea or vomiting, blurred vision, confusion, abnormal heartbeat, or nosebleed. WHO advises immediate care for these symptoms with very high blood pressure.

NEXT STEPS:

Have blood pressure checked by a trained health worker or clinician, especially if you have risk factors.
Do not start, stop, or change blood-pressure medicines without a clinician’s advice.
Ask about salt intake, physical activity, weight, smoking, alcohol, sleep, and other conditions such as diabetes.
Keep a record of home or clinic blood-pressure readings and take it to appointments.

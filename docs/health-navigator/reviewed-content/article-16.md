ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
Nipah virus infection is a serious zoonotic disease that can spread from animals to people and between people. In Bangladesh, IEDCR identifies drinking raw date palm sap as a risk factor because bats may contaminate the sap during collection.
Symptoms can include fever, headache, cough, sore throat, vomiting, muscle pain, breathing difficulty, confusion, drowsiness, reduced consciousness, seizures, and encephalitis. IEDCR advises that people with fever and unconsciousness, convulsions, or breathing difficulty should be admitted to an isolation ward or hospital facility.

IMPORTANT WARNINGS:
Seek emergency hospital care immediately for fever with confusion, unconsciousness, seizures, severe headache, breathing difficulty, or severe vomiting. Do not drink raw date palm sap; IEDCR states that boiled sap or molasses is safe.

NEXT STEPS:

Avoid raw date palm sap, especially during Nipah season.
Wash fruits thoroughly and avoid partially eaten or fallen fruit.
Wash hands with soap and water after contact with a sick person.
If a person has fever with confusion, convulsions, or breathing difficulty, arrange urgent hospital care and inform the healthcare team about possible Nipah exposure.

ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
The sources say that if any danger sign appears, the family should follow their emergency plan and get the woman to emergency care immediately. Signs listed include: fever of 38°C or higher, foul-smelling vaginal discharge, severe headache or blurred vision, decreased or no fetal movement, green or brown fluid leaking from the vagina, high blood pressure, vaginal bleeding, difficulty breathing, convulsions or fainting, and severe abdominal pain. Other lists also include swelling of the face and hands.

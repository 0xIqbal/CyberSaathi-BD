ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
Heart disease includes conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels, such as coronary artery disease, heart failure, and abnormal heart rhythms. A stroke happens when blood flow to part of the brain is suddenly interrupted or a blood vessel in the brain bleeds.
In Bangladesh, IEDCR reports that non-communicable diseases account for more than 55 percent of medically certified facility deaths, and cardiovascular conditions account for 62 percent of NCD deaths. Heart disease has a reported prevalence of 21 percent, while stroke has a reported prevalence of 1 percent in cited national data.

ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
Pneumonia is an infection that affects the lungs and can make breathing difficult. Acute respiratory infections (ARI) include infections of the nose, throat, airways, and lungs; pneumonia is a more serious form of lower respiratory infection. IEDCR’s surveillance system includes pneumonia, severe pneumonia, very severe disease, and severe acute respiratory infection.
Symptoms can include cough, fever, fast or difficult breathing, chest pain, tiredness, and reduced feeding or activity in young children. Severity varies, so a clinician should assess breathing, oxygen level, hydration, and the need for treatment or hospital care.

IMPORTANT WARNINGS:
Seek urgent care for fast or difficult breathing, blue lips or face, chest indrawing in a young child, inability to drink, extreme sleepiness or unconsciousness, convulsions, or worsening symptoms. These may indicate severe respiratory illness and need immediate assessment.

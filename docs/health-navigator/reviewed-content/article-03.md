ENGLISH EXPLANATION:
In rural Bangladesh, three government-supported community health workers operate: Community Health Care Providers (CHCP) and Health Assistants under DGHS, and Family Welfare Assistants under DGFP. The CHCP is based at the community clinic. Community clinics are meant to deliver primary health care, family planning and nutrition services at the grassroots level. Higher-level facilities (upazila health complexes, district and medical college hospitals) and specialist doctors such as OB-GYNs handle complications and referrals.
